SAT Holding Group Milestone
Our Journey – Building a Legacy of Excellence
"From our humble beginnings to becoming a diversified multi-industry leader, the SAT Holdings Group
has always been driven by a single purpose: to empower Sri Lanka through local innovation, sustainable
energy, and resilient infrastructure."

2017– The Birth of a Vision * Milestone: Incorporation of SAT Holdings Pvt Ltd.
Started as a dedicated construction, import, and trading company, laying the foundation for the group’s
logistical and infrastructural strength.

2024 – Committed to Sustainability * Milestone: Founding of SAT Energy Solutions Pvt Ltd

Ventured into the renewable energy sector to address the nation’s growing power needs through clean,
sustainable solar technology.

Successfully commissioned and connected our 500kW rooftop solar plant in Tangalle to the National
Grid. With 775 panels and an annual yield of 800MWh, this project stands as a shining example of our
commitment to a greener Sri Lanka.

2024 – Nurturing the Earth * Milestone: Incorporation of SAT Green Agro Pvt Ltd.

Diversified into the agricultural sector, bringing a modern, technology-driven approach to traditional
farming and produce trading.

2026 – Expanding into Electricals * Milestone: Launch of SAT Electricals Pvt Ltd.

Recognized the need for high-quality, locally accessible technology. We began our journey into the
electricals sector with a focus on precision and reliability.

2026 – Embracing "Made in Sri Lanka" * Milestone: Commencement of Rice Cooker Manufacturing.

Achieved a major milestone by establishing our own manufacturing facility. We launched our flagship
Rice Cookers under the "Made in Sri Lanka" label, proving that local engineering can match global
standards.
